# Living Terrain Roadmap

**Role:** Writing roadmap — what Chelsea should write next to make Living Terrain stronger.  
**Not:** A software roadmap. Not a feature list. Not an interface plan.  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-27

Companion: [`ATLAS_CANONICAL_MAP.md`](./ATLAS_CANONICAL_MAP.md) — the intellectual map this roadmap serves.

---

## Morning question

> What should Chelsea write next to make Living Terrain stronger?

Answer from the **HIGH** list first.  
Prefer deepening existing concepts over inventing new ones.  
Every new piece should strengthen an Atlas journey, deepen a book’s gravity, or clarify a relationship already in the corpus.

---

## Editorial principles

1. Every new essay strengthens the Atlas.  
2. Every book excerpt deepens an existing journey.  
3. Every model clarifies an existing relationship.  
4. Nothing exists in isolation.  
5. Avoid duplicate ideas.  
6. Optimize for coherence over volume.  
7. Do not invent content outside what has already been explored — only extend it.

## V. What not to do

- Do not invent new continents to make the roadmap look fuller.  
- Do not write essays that cannot name which concept and journey they strengthen.  
- Do not prioritize models before the prose they clarify.  
- Do not treat *Signals I'm Watching* as equal weight to full essays.  
- Do not redesign Atlas UI to compensate for thin writing.  
- Do not expand scope into features, accounts, search, or AI.

---

## VI. Maintenance rule

When something is published or excerpted into Atlas:

1. Update [`ATLAS_CANONICAL_MAP.md`](./ATLAS_CANONICAL_MAP.md).  
2. Move the item down or off this priority board.  
3. Ask again: *What should Chelsea write next to make Living Terrain stronger?*

The corpus is the product. Future writing should feel inevitable.
